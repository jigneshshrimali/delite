About Delite

40+ Years of Precision Manufacturing

A family-run ceramic machinery manufacturer, built on four decades of engineering experience in Ahmedabad, India.

Delite Ceramic Machinery Equipment manufactures ball mills, filter presses, agitators, and turnkey ceramic processing equipment from a single facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

What started as a small precision engineering operation has grown into a manufacturer trusted across the ceramic tile, sanitaryware, tableware, and technical ceramics industries — with equipment exported internationally, including to South Africa and Vietnam.

The company remains family-run today, with four decades of combined leadership experience across engineering, production, and sales.

How We Work

Our Process

Advanced Engineering

Every machine is engineered in-house at our Ahmedabad facility, from initial design through to final specification.

Premium Raw Materials

Steel and components are sourced and inspected before entering production, supporting long-lasting machine life.

Precision Manufacturing

Machining and assembly take place at our own facility, under direct supervision from our production managers.

Quality Testing

Machines are checked against specification before leaving our facility, ahead of installation at your site.

Installation & Commissioning

Our team supports installation and commissioning, with ongoing service and spare parts availability.
