Overview

Delite's ball mills are engineered for high-capacity grinding of ceramic bodies, glazes, and slips, with a focus on consistent output and long service life.

Built at our Ahmedabad facility, the ball mill range covers a wide capacity band — verified active listings span from smaller-batch configurations up to 40 ton capacity, suited to both mid-size and large-scale ceramic manufacturing operations.

As with all Delite equipment, exact configuration (drum size, motor rating, grinding media charge) is matched to your specific production requirement — request a quote for a specification tailored to your line.
