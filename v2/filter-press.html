Overview

Delite's filter presses handle dewatering across ceramic slip, mineral processing, and chemical process applications, with plate configurations spanning 470mm to 1500mm.

Verified active configurations include units with up to 50 plates and filtration capacity exceeding 3,000 litres per hour, suited to continuous high-throughput operations.
